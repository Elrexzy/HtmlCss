Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal. This classic omelette is made with eggs and you can add cheese, vegetables or meat if you like.

Preparation time

  • Total: 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • Cheese, vegetables or meat (optional)

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs: Break the eggs into a bowl and add salt and pepper. Mix everything together.
  2. Heat the pan: Put a frying pan on medium heat and add butter or oil.
  3. Cook the eggs: Pour the eggs into the pan and allow them to cook.
  4. Add fillings: Add cheese, vegetables or meat if you want.
  5. Fold the omelette: Fold the omelette in half and allow it to cook for one more minute.
  6. Serve: Put the omelette on a plate and enjoy!

Nutrition

The nutritional information below is for one serving without extra fillings.
